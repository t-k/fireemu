Task: propose test cases derived from a fixed specification excerpt.

Every proposal must be grounded in a specific sentence or clause of the excerpt and must describe both sides of the behavior. For each proposal, the finding is:
- "claim": the proposed test name (snake_case) and a one-sentence statement of the expected behavior.
- "path", "startLine", "endLine": the specification lines the proposal is derived from.
- "evidence": the quoted specification text.
- "positiveControl": the concrete accepted case that must succeed (inputs and the expected successful result).
- "refusalPostState": the concrete refused case and the observable state after the refusal (what was NOT changed, and the error the caller sees).

Do not propose tests for behavior the excerpt does not state; put such ideas into "unknowns".
